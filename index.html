How to Play

Goal

Move all four pieces around the board and finish before the other teams.

Throw values

Do = 1, Gae = 2, Geol = 3, Yut = 4, Mo = 5. Yut and Mo give another throw.

Move

Select a stored result, then select a highlighted piece. Your own pieces stack; landing on an opponent sends it home.

Photos

Each player can replace the default photo from the setup page. The choice is saved in that browser.
